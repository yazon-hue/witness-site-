HOW IT WORKS

Don't reward noise. Reward the truth.

WITNESS turns public blockchain data into reviewed intelligence. Anyone can investigate. Only findings that hold up earn reputation.

01

Investigate

Paste any Solana wallet or transaction into Explore. Follow funds, see counterparties and token holdings.

02

Submit evidence

Publish a finding with transaction signatures attached. Each one is checked against Solana automatically.

03

Community review

Other investigators confirm or challenge the reasoning. Nobody can review their own case.

04

Earn reputation

Verified findings earn reputation. Findings that fall apart under challenge cost it.

Three kinds of claim, never mixed up.

Every investigation is split into labelled layers, so readers always know which statements are facts and which are opinions.

CONFIRMED
Verifiable on-chain dataTransaction signatures, checked automatically. Each is flagged if it isn't found, or if the investigated wallet isn't part of it.
INTERPRETATION
What the investigator thinks it meansMust rest on the confirmed data. Reviewers judge the reasoning.
SPECULATION
Open questions and hunchesAlways labelled. Never shown as fact, and never earns reputation on its own.

How status and reputation move

Reputation is earned slowly and lost quickly. That keeps the incentive pointed at accuracy, not attention.
